# Matcha & Emoji — Collection 02

24 animated concepts: 12 matcha cat stickers and 12 classic emoji.
Open index.html to review, pause, switch backgrounds or download an individual image.

Each cycle lasts 4 seconds and is sampled at 25 frames per second.
Continuous local mesh motion animates the approved artwork: paws, tail, head,
breathing, heartbeat and reaction-specific movement. Each animation uses one
selected art pose. Faces are not re-drawn frame by frame. No optical-flow
crossfades remain in the deliverable: those produced doubled contours and were
discarded. This is a motion concept collection, not production character rigging.

WebP: 320 x 320, generated transparency retained, 25 fps, 4-second loop.
GIF: 240 x 240, ivory background, 25 fps, 4-second loop.
PNG: first frame, 320 x 320, transparent.
Encoders can combine identical frames while preserving total timing.

Cat: wave, laugh, love, sleep, approval, shy, sad, angry, party, surprise,
matcha tea break, cool.
Emoji: smile, laugh, heart, kiss, love eyes, surprise, sad, angry, thumbs up,
clap, fire, party.

The art was generated with built-in ImageGen; prompts.json records the new
sheet prompts. The original eight designs are reused from Collection 01.
This is a standalone visual preview; no Flutter app integration is included.
validation.json records full-frame decoding, dimensions and duration checks.
